**Title:** AI Customer Support Agent for Shopify: Answers From Your Docs, Hands Off to Humans

**Description:** Answers product, shipping and order questions 24/7 using only the store's own help articles, and passes refunds, damage reports and complaints to the team with a summary. Tested on 40 customer questions: 100% answered correctly with a source, 100% of out-of-scope questions correctly declined, 5/5 escalations routed, median reply 11 seconds. Built with n8n, Claude and OpenAI (tested on both), Supabase vector search and the Shopify API.
