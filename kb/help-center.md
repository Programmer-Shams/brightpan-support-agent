# Brightpan Kitchen Supply — Help Center

Brightpan Kitchen Supply is an online store. It sells cookware, knives and small kitchen electrics, and ships from Ohio, USA.

Product lines: Everyday (nonstick), Classic (3-ply stainless steel), Pro (5-ply stainless steel), Cast Iron, Lite (aluminum), Edge knives, and electrics (Brightpan Kettle, Brightpan Hand Blender).

## [A01] Shipping options and costs (United States)

We ship to all 50 US states.

- Standard shipping (contiguous US): 3 to 5 business days, $6.95. Free on orders of $75 or more, calculated after discounts and before tax.
- Express shipping (contiguous US): 1 to 2 business days, $14.95. Express is never free.
- Alaska and Hawaii: standard shipping only, 5 to 8 business days, $12.95 flat. Free shipping does not apply to Alaska and Hawaii.

Orders placed before 1:00 pm Eastern Time, Monday to Friday, ship the same business day. Orders placed after 1:00 pm Eastern Time, or on a weekend or US public holiday, ship the next business day.

## [A02] International shipping

We ship to Canada and the United Kingdom only. We do not ship to any other country.

- Canada: 7 to 12 business days, $19.95.
- United Kingdom: 8 to 14 business days, $24.95.

Import duties and taxes are not included in the price. The customer pays them to the carrier on delivery. Free shipping does not apply to international orders.

## [A03] Tracking your order

When your order ships, we email you a tracking number. Standard and express US orders ship with UPS. Alaska, Hawaii and PO Box orders ship with USPS. Canada and UK orders ship with DHL.

Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan. You can also check your order at any time on the Order Status page by entering your order number and the email address used at checkout.

## [A04] Changing or cancelling an order

You can change or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it, using the link in your order confirmation email or by contacting our support team.

After 1 hour, the order goes to our warehouse and can no longer be changed or cancelled online. Our support team will check whether it can still be stopped. If the order has already shipped, it cannot be cancelled. Once it is delivered, you can return it under our returns policy.

## [A05] Returns policy

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery.

- Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
- Knives must be unused and unsharpened.
- Final sale items cannot be returned: clearance items, gift cards and engraved or personalized items.

To start a return, go to the Returns Portal and enter your order number and the email address used at checkout.

US returns use a prepaid return label. A $7.50 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund, unless the item arrived damaged, defective or wrong. Canada and UK customers pay their own return shipping.

## [A06] Refunds

Once your return arrives at our warehouse and passes inspection, we refund your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Original shipping charges are not refunded, unless the return is because of our error (a damaged, defective or wrong item).

If you prefer, you can choose store credit instead of a refund. Store credit is issued within 1 business day of the return being inspected.

Refunds are issued by our support team. They cannot be issued through chat.

## [A07] Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. To swap an item for a different size or color, return the original item through the Returns Portal and place a new order.

## [A08] Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or wrong, tell us within 7 days of delivery. Include your order number and photos of the item and the packaging.

We will send a free replacement or a full refund, including shipping, and you will not need to pay return shipping. Damaged and wrong-item reports are handled by our support team.

## [A09] Warranty

- Classic, Pro and Cast Iron cookware: lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects.

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, misuse, overheating, or damage from putting nonstick pans or knives in the dishwasher.

To make a warranty claim, contact our support team with your order number and photos of the problem. Warranty claims are reviewed by our support team.

## [A10] Caring for Everyday nonstick pans

- Hand wash only. Everyday pans are not dishwasher safe, and dishwasher damage is not covered by the warranty.
- Use wooden, silicone or nylon utensils. Do not use metal utensils.
- Cook on low to medium heat. High heat damages the coating.
- Oven safe up to 400°F without the lid. Glass lids are oven safe up to 350°F.

## [A11] Caring for Classic and Pro stainless steel

- Dishwasher safe, but hand washing keeps the shine longer.
- For rainbow discoloration or white spots, use a non-abrasive stainless steel cleaner.
- Oven safe up to 500°F, including stainless steel lids.
- Works on all cooktops, including induction.

## [A12] Caring for Cast Iron

- Cast Iron pans come pre-seasoned and are ready to use.
- Hand wash only. Dry immediately and rub on a thin layer of cooking oil.
- Not dishwasher safe.
- Oven safe up to 650°F. Works on all cooktops, including induction.

## [A13] Caring for Edge knives

- Hand wash and dry right away. Do not put knives in the dishwasher.
- Hone the edge about once a week and sharpen once or twice a year.
- Edge knives have a 15-degree edge on each side.

## [A14] Lid compatibility

- Brightpan Universal Lids come in 8-inch, 10-inch and 12-inch sizes. Each fits Everyday, Classic and Pro skillets of the same size.
- Universal Lids do not fit Cast Iron skillets. Cast Iron lids are sold separately.
- Lite pans use their own Lite lids.

## [A15] Induction compatibility

- Induction compatible: Classic, Pro, Cast Iron and Everyday (Everyday pans have a steel base).
- Not induction compatible: Lite (aluminum).
